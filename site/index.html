The presets use the minimum round lengths for Championship Series events. BO3 time is for the whole match, not each game.

When time is called

Finish the current turn, then play 3 additional turns, starting with the opponent. If time is called during Pokémon Checkup, the next turn is turn 1.

The 15-minute overtime clock starts immediately and simultaneously for every table. It is a maximum, not extra time to use after the additional turns. Report results as soon as your match resolves.

Turns are tracked at each table, not on this shared clock. Individual judge-granted time extensions and Swiss / single-elimination tiebreakers must be handled by the judges.
